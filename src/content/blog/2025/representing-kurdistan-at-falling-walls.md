Seven months ago, I had the honor of representing Kurdistan on a global stage in Berlin at the [Falling Walls Foundation](https://www.linkedin.com/company/falling-walls-foundation/) Science Summit.

It was an unforgettable experience, presenting my project to an audience of participants and speakers from more than 60 countries. We explored Berlin together, exchanged ideas across cultures, and connected with inspiring people from around the world.

Beyond sharing projects, we built networks that can grow into future collaborations and businesses.

I want to sincerely thank [Open Space Academy](https://www.linkedin.com/company/openspaceac/), the [Kurdistan Foundation](https://www.linkedin.com/company/kurdistanfoundation/), [Erbil Polytechnic University](https://www.linkedin.com/company/erbil-polytechnic-university/), [Salahaddin University-Erbil](https://www.linkedin.com/company/salahaddin-university-erbil/), and all the sponsors who made this incredible experience possible.
